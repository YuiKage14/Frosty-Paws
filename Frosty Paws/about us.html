About Us

Frosty Paws ice cream was launched in late 60s at the Mall Road, Lahore. It became an instant success and within no time, Frosty Paws was one of the most loved ice cream bands in Pakistan. Frosty Paws ice cream started its operations in Lahore and since then it has served our esteemed customers with love and dedication. As a result, Frosty Paws ice creams popularity has grown throughout the years
